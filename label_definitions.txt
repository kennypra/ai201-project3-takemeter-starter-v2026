showcase: the poster presents a dish they made, with or without a description of how they made it, and does not ask anyone to judge it or ask the community a question
rate_request: the poster shares a dish they made and explicitly asks others to rate, judge, or critique it ("rate my", "smash or pass", "critique", "what can I improve")
question: the poster asks the community for information, advice, ideas, or opinions about anything other than a verdict on a dish they made
